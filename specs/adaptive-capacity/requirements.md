| `ARBORA-CAPACITY-R001` | **AIMD error-budget throttling that only narrows automatically (rollup).** Subsystem: fleet admission, capacity. Entry: every served `fleet.call` records an outcome, and the controller loop started at host bootstrap closes windows. Arbora observes a bounded error budget per tenant, child and operation class. It applies additive-increase and multiplicative-decrease throttling under the engine's declared capacity cell ceiling. An automatic action may only narrow concurrency or give back what it narrowed, and never exceeds the ceiling. Every decision records its window digest, old and new limits and reason. Children: `.1` pure rule, `.2` joint per-run adjustment, `.3` closed-loop capacity proposals, `.4` outcome capture and controller, `.5` admission limiter. `capacity.adjust.apply` is the served actuation operation. Depends on: `MEDULLA-PLACEMENT-R001.15`. Caller chain: every served `fleet.call` → `arbora/fleet/throttle_service.py` outcome recorder → controller loop started at host bootstrap → `arbora/fleet/error_budget.py::decide` → admission boundary. Shape: `ThrottleDecision { old_limit, new_limit, mode, reason, window_digest, policy_version }` from `arbora/fleet/error_budget.py`, applied through `arbora/fleet/throttle_service.py`. | Root journey: `tests/fleet/test_throttle_controller_served.py::test_breach_narrows_recovery_is_bounded_and_replay_is_idempotent` (L3). Children pass.  |
| `ARBORA-CAPACITY-R001.1` | **Pure partitioned AIMD decision rule.** `arbora/fleet/error_budget.py` defines typed `Partition`, `OutcomeSample`, `BudgetWindow`, `AimdConfig`, `ThrottleMode` and `ThrottleDecision`, and a pure `decide()` function that computes the next concurrency limit from a closed window and the controller's prior state: eligible service failures (not caller errors or policy denials) drive the error fraction, breach multiplicatively decreases bounded by a floor, a healthy window additively increases bounded by the engine/policy ceiling, and replaying the same window is idempotent. No clock, I/O or fleet dispatch wiring. Caller chain: `arbora/fleet/throttle_service.py` window close → `arbora/fleet/error_budget.py::decide`. Shape: the types `Partition`, `OutcomeSample`, `BudgetWindow`, `AimdConfig`, `ThrottleMode`, `ThrottleDecision` and the pure `decide()` in `arbora/fleet/error_budget.py`. | Test: `tests/fleet/test_error_budget.py::test_decide_decrease_increase_bounds_and_idempotence`. `tests/fleet/test_error_budget.py` covers bounded recovery, multiplicative decrease, the floor and engine-headroom ceiling, caller-error/policy-denial neutrality, insufficient-sample handling, idempotent replay, and tenant/child partition isolation.  |
